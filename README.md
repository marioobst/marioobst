## Personal web projects

All projects are personally run and non-commercial. They give me room to keep learning on my own. Working with AI is an important part of that, and AI-generated images are labelled on the respective sites.

| Project | What it is about |
|---|---|
| [prompt-manual.fyi](https://prompt-manual.fyi/) | A free, independent reference for working with AI. Developed through conversation, practical experiments, and learning by doing. Available in English and German. [Project documentation](https://github.com/marioobst/prompt-manual). |
| [erfurt.life](https://erfurt.life) | City history, my own photographs, and personal connections, in several languages |
| [evolution.surf](https://evolution.surf/en/) | Stories and interactive activities about change in landscapes, life, society and everyday objects, in English and German. [Project documentation](https://github.com/marioobst/evolution-surf). |
| [insekten.website](https://insekten.website) | The world of insects through Earth's history, topics, and guided tours |
| [planeten.website](https://planeten.website) | Astronomy with interactive learning models |
| [politik.buzz](https://politik.buzz) | A growing knowledge project on political history |
| [mario-aik.space](https://mario-aik.space/) | Our shared home for projects, workshop journals and lessons from working together. It now includes the former mario-obst.fyi journal. [Project documentation](https://github.com/marioobst/mario-aik-space). |
| [wanderlust.wiki](https://wanderlust.wiki/en/) | A nature journal with photography, practical companions and places to discover at your own pace, in English and German. |
| [stasi1989.com](https://stasi1989.com/) | My personal account of imprisonment in East Germany, with historical documents and present-day eyewitness work. |

Explore the [complete project directory](https://mario-aik.space/projects/) for more websites, tools and experiments.

## Where I come from

- **ALSO Group**, Head of Digital Press (2010–2022): digital corporate communications across Europe, international web presences.
- **7 layers AG**, Web & Digital Communications (1999–2010): web platforms for North America, Europe, and Asia, SEO, SEA, and analytics.

I wrote my first websites myself in the 1990s. The question of how content can be presented and conveyed on the internet has stayed with me ever since.

## Volunteering in Erfurt

- **Honorary judge** at the Erfurt Regional Court (Landgericht Erfurt), since 2024.
- **Volunteer educational work** with the Stiftung Ettersberg at the [Andreasstraße](https://andreasstrasse.de) Memorial and Education Centre. As a contemporary witness, I talk with groups about my experiences and the meaning of freedom.
- **Preserving memory:** On [stasi1989.com](https://stasi1989.com/) I document my 1989 imprisonment by the Stasi, distinguishing between my own recollection, what the files state, and historical context.
